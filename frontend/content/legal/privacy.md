# NEXUS Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 7, 2026

NEXUS is a volunteer management tool for Science Olympiad tournaments. It is run by its maintainers, listed on the open-source [NEXUS GitHub repository](https://github.com/ethnjs/nexus) ("we", "us"), and is available at nexus.socalscioly.org (the "Service"). This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, who can see it, and the choices you have.

NEXUS is an independent project. It is not operated or endorsed by Science Olympiad, Inc.

## Who is responsible for your information

There are two kinds of people who handle your information on NEXUS:

- **Us, the maintainers.** We run the Service, store the data, and are responsible for this policy.
- **Organizers.** Tournament directors and tournament staff ("Organizers") use NEXUS to run their own tournaments. When you join a tournament, its Organizers can see and use your information to staff it. Organizers decide what their forms ask and how they use your answers. If you have questions about how a particular tournament uses your information, contact its Organizers directly.

## Information we collect

### Information you give us

- **Account information:** your name, email address, and password. We store your password only as a one-way hash and never see it in plain text. We also record which version of our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy you accepted, and when.
- **Profile information:** phone number, date of birth, pronouns, school, major, student status, year and graduation year, employer, T-shirt size, dietary restrictions, and your Science Olympiad competition and volunteering history. Some fields are optional.
- **Tournament information:** the tournaments you join, your roles, your availability, event preferences, lunch choices, and the shifts and events you are assigned to.
- **Form responses:** your answers to forms that Organizers create. Organizers choose the questions, so a form may ask for information not listed here. We do not review forms and are not responsible for what they ask. Never enter passwords, Social Security numbers, bank or credit card details, or other sensitive information into a form, even if it asks.

### Information collected automatically

- **Session information:** when you sign in, we record the IP address and browser for that session, along with when it was created and last used. You can see and sign out of your active sessions from your account settings.
- **Activity records:** NEXUS keeps an audit log of changes made within a tournament (for example, role changes, join code updates, staff invitations, and data exports) and who made them. Some entries include names or email addresses.
- **Usage analytics:** we use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and understand how the Service is used. It does not use cookies and does not identify you personally.
- **Server logs:** like most websites, our hosting providers automatically log requests to the Service, including your IP address, browser, and the pages you request. We use these logs only to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Service.

### Information about people who are not users

When an Organizer invites someone to help run a tournament, we receive that person's email address to send the invitation, and the tournament's audit log keeps it.

### Cookies and local storage

NEXUS uses one essential cookie, `access_token`, to keep you signed in. It expires after 7 days or when you sign out.

NEXUS also saves a few display preferences in your browser's local storage. These stay on your device and are not sent to us.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use third-party tracking tools.

## How we use your information

We use your information to:

- create and secure your account, including verifying your email and resetting your password;
- let you join tournaments and let their Organizers staff them;
- send you emails about your account and the tournaments you take part in;
- keep the Service working, secure, and free of abuse; and
- understand how the Service is used so we can improve it.

We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Some information, such as dietary restrictions or pronouns, may reveal details about your health, religion, or gender identity. We use it only to run the tournaments you take part in, and we do not use it to infer anything about you.

## How we respond to Do Not Track

Some browsers can send a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal. NEXUS does not track you across other websites, and we do not allow third parties to collect information about your activity over time and across different websites through the Service. Because there is no such tracking to turn off, NEXUS works the same way whether or not your browser sends these signals.

## Who can see your information

- **Only you** can see your date of birth and your session information (IP addresses and browsers). They are not shown to Organizers or to NEXUS administrators anywhere in the Service.
- **Organizers of the tournaments you join** can see the rest of your profile, your responses to their forms, and your assignments. What each Organizer can see depends on the role they hold. Organizers can also export this information (for example, to a spreadsheet) to run their tournament, and each export is recorded in the tournament's audit log.
- **Other volunteers** in the same tournament may see limited information such as your name and assignments where needed for coordination.
- **NEXUS administrators** can view and manage accounts through the Service's admin tools, apart from the information listed above as visible only to you.
- **Service providers** that host or support NEXUS, under their own privacy and security terms:
  - Railway (backend hosting and database)
  - Vercel (website hosting and analytics)
  - Resend (sending email)
- **Legal reasons:** we may disclose information if required by law, or if we believe in good faith it is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, the public, or us.

Because we operate the database, the maintainers may access stored data directly when needed to maintain the Service, fix problems, or respond to legal requests. We do not otherwise access it.

Organizers who export your information must follow the rules in our [Terms of Service](/terms#3-organizers-and-volunteer-information). They may use it only to run their tournament, must keep it secure, may share it only with people helping run the tournament, and must delete it when it is no longer needed or when they receive a request to delete your information. Once a copy leaves NEXUS, we cannot technically control it, but we may suspend Organizers who break these rules.

## Age disclosure to tournaments

Some tournaments need to know whether volunteers are over 18 or 21. When you join one of these tournaments, NEXUS asks for your consent before sharing an over-18 or over-21 indicator, calculated from your date of birth as of the tournament's first day. The tournament sees only the indicator, never your date of birth.

## Children and minors

NEXUS is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, we will delete it. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us at [support@nexus.socalscioly.org](mailto:support@nexus.socalscioly.org).

Users aged 13 to 17 may use NEXUS only with the permission of a parent or guardian.

If you are under 18, you can remove information you have posted on NEXUS by editing your profile, leaving a tournament, or deleting your account, or you can ask us to remove it by emailing [support@nexus.socalscioly.org](mailto:support@nexus.socalscioly.org). Removal does not guarantee that every copy is gone: for example, copies Organizers exported earlier and audit log entries may remain.

## Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information for as long as your account exists.

- **Deactivating** your account (in account settings) signs you out and blocks sign-in, but keeps your information. Contact us to reactivate it.
- **Deleting** your account (in account settings) permanently removes your profile, experience history, sessions, tournament memberships, and form responses. Things you created for others, such as tournaments and forms, remain, but are no longer linked to your account. For security and compliance purposes, tournament audit log entries also remain, and may include your name and email address.

If we keep database backups, deleted data may remain in them until those backups expire.

Deleting your account does not delete copies of your information that Organizers exported before then. To have those copies deleted, email [support@nexus.socalscioly.org](mailto:support@nexus.socalscioly.org), and we will forward your request to the Organizers of the tournaments you were part of.

## Your choices

- You can view and edit your profile at any time from your profile and settings pages.
- You can leave a tournament at any time before it is archived. Leaving removes your membership and your responses to that tournament's forms, and its Organizers can no longer see your information in NEXUS. To have copies they exported earlier deleted, email us and we will forward your request.
- You can delete your account at any time.
- You can contact us at [support@nexus.socalscioly.org](mailto:support@nexus.socalscioly.org) to ask for a copy of your information or to ask us to correct or delete it.

## Your California privacy rights

California law gives California residents specific privacy rights. NEXUS is a free, non-commercial project and may not be required to follow every part of these laws, but we give these rights to all users, wherever they live.

### Categories of personal information

In the past 12 months, we have collected the following categories of personal information, from you, from the Organizers of tournaments you join, and automatically as you use the Service:

- **Identifiers:** name, email address, phone number, IP address, and account ID.
- **Personal characteristics:** date of birth, age indicators calculated from it, and pronouns.
- **Education and employment information:** school, major, student status, year, graduation year, and employer.
- **Internet activity:** session records, server logs, audit log entries, and page view analytics.
- **Other information you provide:** T-shirt size, dietary restrictions, availability, event preferences, Science Olympiad experience, and form responses.

We collect this information for the purposes in "[How we use your information](#how-we-use-your-information)", disclose it only as described in "[Who can see your information](#who-can-see-your-information)", and keep it as described in "[Keeping and deleting your information](#keeping-and-deleting-your-information)".

We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we have not done so in the past 12 months. We do not sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their direct marketing purposes.

### Your rights

You can ask us to:

- tell you what personal information we have collected about you, where it came from, why we collected it, and who we disclosed it to, and give you a copy of it;
- delete your personal information, subject to the exceptions described in "[Keeping and deleting your information](#keeping-and-deleting-your-information)"; and
- correct personal information that is inaccurate.

You can do much of this yourself from your profile and account settings. For anything else, email [support@nexus.socalscioly.org](mailto:support@nexus.socalscioly.org). We will respond within 45 days. To verify your identity, we may ask you to send the request from the email address on your account. You may have an authorized agent make a request for you, and we may ask for proof that you authorized them.

We will not treat you differently for exercising any of these rights.

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including hashed passwords, encrypted connections, and hashed session and verification tokens. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Where your information is stored

NEXUS is operated from the United States, and your information is stored and processed in the United States.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you by email or through the Service before they take effect. The date at the top shows when this policy was last updated.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to [support@nexus.socalscioly.org](mailto:support@nexus.socalscioly.org).
