---

NEXUS is a tournament management platform for Science Olympiad. Tournament directors use it to
set up a tournament, collect volunteer sign-ups, and assign people to events and shifts.

## Joining a tournament

Every tournament has a join code. Open the app, create an account, and enter the code to join as a
volunteer — you'll be asked to fill in a short profile first.

## Running a tournament

A tournament's setup checklist walks through the pieces in order: tracks, events, shifts, and the
volunteer sign-up form. Each step links to the page that completes it.
