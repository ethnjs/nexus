---
title: Edit your form responses
description: View what you submitted on a tournament form and change your answers.
---

After you submit a tournament form, you can review your answers at any time. If the tournament director allows it, you can also change them.

1. Open the tournament and go to **Overview**.
2. In the **Forms** card, find the form. Forms you've submitted are marked **Completed**.
3. Optional: To review your answers, click the form's row. Your answers open read-only in a new tab.
   - Questions you skipped show **Not answered**.
   - If the director removed a choice you picked, it still shows, marked **Removed**.
4. Next to the form, or at the top of your answers, click **Edit**.
   - If you see a **Locked** badge, you can't change this form right now. To find out why, point to the badge:
     - **Editing is locked:** The director hasn't turned on edits for this form. To change something, contact the director.
     - **This form isn't accepting changes right now:** The form is closed or the tournament is archived.
5. The form opens one question at a time, with your answers filled in. Update the answers you want to change. To keep an answer, click **Continue**.
6. At the end of the form, click **Submit**.
