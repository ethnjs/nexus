---
title: How to edit your form responses
description: View what you submitted on a tournament form and change your answers.
---

After you submit a tournament form, you can look back at your answers at any time. If the tournament director allows it, you can also change them.

## See your answers

1. Open the tournament and go to its **Overview**.
2. In the **Forms** card, find the form. Forms you've submitted are marked **Completed**.
3. Click the form's row. Your answers open in a new tab, read-only.

Questions you skipped show **Not answered**. If the director has since removed a choice you picked, it still shows, marked **Removed**.

## Change your answers

1. Click **Edit** next to the form, either in the **Forms** card or at the top of your answers.
2. The form opens one question at a time, with your previous answers already filled in.
3. Click **Continue** to move past questions you want to keep, and change the ones you don't.
4. When you reach the end, click **Submit**.

Only the answers you changed are saved. Everything else stays as it was.

To leave without saving, use your browser's back button.

## If Edit is greyed out

A **Locked** badge next to **Edit** means you can't change this form right now. Hover over the badge to see why:

- **Editing is locked.** The tournament director hasn't turned on edits for this form. Contact them if something needs changing.
- **This form isn't accepting changes right now.** The form has been closed or the tournament has been archived.
