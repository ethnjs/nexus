A small fix for tournaments that collect age flags but don't have a date yet.

## Fixes

- Every member of a tournament with TBD dates showed **18+ Unknown** and **21+ Unknown**, even after consenting. Until a date is set, age is now checked against today's date.

## Worth knowing

- **Age flags on a TBD tournament are provisional.** Once you set the first date, they're rechecked against that day, so someone who turns 18 before the tournament will switch from Under 18 to 18+.
