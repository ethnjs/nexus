You can now get your data out of NEXUS and into the tools you hand them off to. A new **Export** button on the members and assignments pages builds TORUS and Duosmium uploads, email lists, and your own saved presets.

## What's new

### The Export button

**Export** sits in the toolbar on the **Members** and **Assignments** pages.

- **Click Export** to open the export screen, where you can preview and adjust an export before you use it.
- **Open the arrow beside it** to run an export in one click, straight from the menu.
- Built-in exports include everyone, and saved exports use the filters saved with them.
- Copy the result to paste somewhere, or download it as a CSV or TXT file.

### TORUS and Duosmium exports

NEXUS can build the lists TORUS and Duosmium ask for, ready to paste in.

- **TORUS:** pick a track and a TORUS role. Test writers and reviewers are listed once per event they're assigned to. Tournament directors and test coordinators are listed by email.
- **Duosmium:** pick a track and a division. Tournament directors, scoremasters and event supervisors are listed together, with each supervisor's events.

To use these, tell NEXUS what each of your roles is in TORUS and Duosmium. Open `Settings → Roles`, pick a role, and choose its `TORUS role` and `Duosmium role`. If you pick a TORUS or Duosmium role that none of your roles are mapped to, the export screen shows a red banner with a link to role settings.

### Email list

**Email list** gives you every member's email on one line, separated by commas, ready to paste into the "To" field of an email.

### Custom exports

Build your own export when the built-in ones don't fit, and save it for the whole staff to reuse.

- **Per member or per event:** choose whether each row is a person, or an event with everyone assigned to it.
- **Columns:** pick from contact details, roles, tracks, availability, lunch, event preferences, assignments and form responses. Shifts can be written as names or as times.
- **Sort** by any of the columns you added, and turn the header row on or off.
- **Export type:** choose whether one click copies it or downloads a CSV or TXT file.
- **Filters:** narrow who's included with member and event filters. They're saved with the export, so it covers the same people every time you run it.
- Saved exports appear under **Saved** in the Export menu for everyone on staff who can manage members.

The export screen has its own member and event filters, and starts with none set. Changing them there doesn't change the page behind it.

## Improvements

- **Pop-ups open and close with a smooth grow animation.**

## Worth knowing

- **Exporting needs the Manage members permission.** On the assignments page, staff who can only manage events will see the Export button locked.
- **Existing roles start out unmapped.** TORUS and Duosmium exports are empty until you set those roles in **Settings → Roles**. Loading the default roles maps them for you.
