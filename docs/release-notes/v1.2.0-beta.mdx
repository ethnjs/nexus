Tournament directors no longer have to invent a date or venue they don't have yet, and can stop volunteers changing their answers once a day is staffed.

## What's new

### Dates and venues can be TBD

A tournament often exists before its venue is booked or its second day is scheduled. You can now say so instead of typing in a placeholder.

- When creating a tournament or a track, tick **Date not decided yet** or **Venue not decided yet**, and it shows as **TBD** everywhere.
- Leaving a date or venue blank is still an error. TBD has to be ticked on purpose.
- A day with no date yet can't have shifts. Set the date first, then add them.

### Closing a track to changes

Once you've staffed a day from people's answers, a volunteer quietly changing their availability can undo your planning. You can now lock a track.

- On a locked track, volunteers can't change their availability, lunch, event preferences or whether they're coming — backing out included.
- **New tracks start locked.** Turn member editing on when you're ready for it, or use **Lock all tracks** in tournament settings to close everything at once.
- Volunteers still see everything they answered, with a note that the track is closed and to contact the tournament director.
- Signup forms keep working on a locked track, so people can still sign up.

## Improvements

- **Creating a tournament is now two steps**: details first, then date and location.

## Fixes

- A pop-up window taller than your screen could hide its own title and buttons. It now scrolls with the page.
- You can no longer clear a day's date while it still has shifts, which left those shifts attached to no date at all.
- Tightened how your sign-in is protected against requests from other websites.

## Worth knowing

- **Volunteers answer a track for the first time through its signup form.** Editing your member profile is for changing an answer afterwards.
- **Tracks that already exist are not locked by this update.** Only tracks created from now on start locked.
- **Locking a track doesn't stop signup forms.** A form with questions about a locked track still records answers — unpublish the form as well if you want the track fully closed.
