Tournament directors can now read every form response and decide whether members may change theirs.

## What's new

### See every response to a form

Each form now has a **Responses** tab next to **Questions**.

- Pick a member from the list to see their answers exactly as they gave them.
- Search the list by name or email.
- Questions someone skipped show **Not answered**. An option that has been removed from the form question still shows, marked **Removed**.
- **Delete** removes one member's response, for example a test submission or one that needs redoing.

### Let members edit their responses

Each form has an **Allow response edits** switch in its `⋮` menu, in the form builder and on the forms list. It starts off.

- With it on, members can open a form they've submitted and change any answer.
- With it off, the form shows a **Locked** badge on your forms list, and members see their answers but can't change them.
- When a member edits, only the answers they actually changed are saved.

### Members can see what they submitted

On the tournament overview, clicking a completed form in the **Forms** card now opens your answers, read-only.

- **Edit** next to it lets you change them, when the form allows it.
- When it doesn't, Edit is greyed out with a **Locked** badge saying to contact your tournament director.
- Editing goes one question at a time with your previous answers already filled in, so you can click straight through to the one you want to change. See [How to edit your form responses](/docs/nexus/volunteer-guides/edit-form-responses).

## Improvements

- **Forms list rows are tidier.** Edit, Preview, View responses and Allow response edits moved into a `⋮` menu; publishing, archiving and deleting stay on the row.
- **Ranked-choice questions preview every option** in the form builder, not just the first few.
- **Deleting a response is recorded** in the tournament's audit log, with the member's name and the form.

## Fixes

- Members could open the form builder for a form they could fill out. They couldn't save anything, but they now see a **No access** page instead.

### Deleting an account

Admins can now delete any account. Before, deleting someone who had answered a form failed.

- Deleting someone removes everything personal: their profile, experience, tournament memberships and form responses.
- Tournaments they own are kept, without an owner, so the tournament's history stays. The delete confirmation lists them, and an admin can give each one a new owner from its settings with **Transfer ownership**.
- Forms, invites and audit log entries they made are kept, and show **Deleted user** where their name was.
- You now type **DELETE** to confirm.

## Worth knowing

- **Deleting a response keeps what it filled in elsewhere.** Availability, lunch, event preferences and track status stay on the member's profile.
- **Admins deleting an account can't be undone.** Lock the account instead if you only need to cut off access.
- **Existing forms start with edits off.** Turn **Allow response edits** on per form.
- **Locked tracks don't stop form edits.** A member editing their form response can still change answers about a track you've locked. Turn **Allow response edits** off as well if you want those answers fixed.
